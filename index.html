Classical Political Economy 1,200+ Words

Key Economic Concepts of Economics in One Lesson (Henry Hazlitt, 1946)

A thorough analytical study of Henry Hazlitt's classic 1946 economic masterpiece. Examines the fundamental axiom of looking beyond immediate special-interest benefits to recognize indirect, long-term macroeconomic impacts on the entire populace.

  • The Broken Window Fallacy & unseen opportunity costs
  • The illusion of wealth creation via public works and credit subsidies
  • The technological unemployment myth and capital accumulation
  • Distortions caused by price fixing, minimum wage, and rent ceilings

Game Theory: Foundations, Strategic Equilibria & Economic Applications

A rigorous exploration of mathematical game theory and strategic interaction. Details how rational agents make interdependent choices across simultaneous and sequential environments, with direct applications to oligopoly pricing, signaling, and auction design.

  • Normal vs. Extensive form games & backward induction
  • The Prisoner's Dilemma & Pareto sub-optimal outcomes
  • Nash Equilibrium (pure vs. mixed strategies) and existence
  • Repeated games, Tit-for-Tat, and the Folk Theorem
  • Information asymmetry, signaling, and mechanism design
